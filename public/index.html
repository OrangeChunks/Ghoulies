How to Play Ghoulies

Objective

End each round with the lowest points. The game ends when a player hits 51+ points — that player loses. If both exceed 51, the highest score loses.

Card Values

A = 1 pt
2 – 10 = face
J / Q = 10 pts
K ♠ / K ♣ = 10 pts
K ♥ / K ♦ = 0 pts

Starting a Round

  • 4 cards each, face-down.
  • Peek at 2 of your own — remember them, they go back down.
  • One card starts the discard pile.

Your Turn

  • Draw from the deck or take the top discard.
  • Swap it with a hand card, or discard it.

Snap!

Double-tap a hand card whose value matches the top discard to snap it off your hand. Miss? Skip a turn.

Special 10

Discard a 10 from your hand to swap any of your cards with any of your opponent's — both stay face-down.

Call Ghoulies!

Think you have the lowest hand? Call it. Opponent gets one final turn.

  • Your hand IS lower → −5 bonus
  • Your hand is NOT lower → +5 penalty
