🂱 How to Play Ghoulies

🎯 Objective

End each round with the lowest points possible. The game ends when a player hits 51+ points — that player loses. If both exceed 51, the highest score loses.

🃏 Card Values

A = 1 pt
2–10 = face value
J / Q = 10 pts
K (black) = 10 pts
K♥ / K♦ = 0 pts!

🔄 Starting the Round

  • Each player starts with 4 cards face-down.
  • You may secretly peek at 2 of your own cards — then they go face-down again. You cannot check again.
  • One card is flipped face-up to start the discard pile. The rest form the draw pile.

🔁 Your Turn

  • Draw from the deck or take the top discard.
  • Either swap the drawn card with one in your hand (the swapped-out card goes to the discard), or discard it directly.

⚡ Snap!

Double-click one of your cards at any time. If its value matches the top card on the discard pile, it snaps onto the pile — removing it from your hand. Miss? You skip your next turn.

🔟 Special 10

When you discard a 10 (any suit) from your hand, you may swap any one of your cards with any one of your opponent's cards. Both cards stay face-down throughout.

👻 Call Ghoulies!

If you think you have the lowest hand, click Call Ghoulies. Your opponent gets one final turn, then the round ends.

  • If your hand is lower: you get a −5 bonus.
  • If your hand isn't lower: you get a +5 penalty.
